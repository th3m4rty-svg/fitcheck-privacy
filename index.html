Privacy Policy for FitCheck Wardrobe

Effective date: October 22, 2026

FitCheck Wardrobe ("FitCheck", "we", "our", or "the app") is an iOS application that allows users to organize clothing, create outfits, and use AI-powered virtual try-on features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use FitCheck Wardrobe, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Process

Photos and Images

FitCheck Wardrobe allows you to select or create:

  • photos of clothing items,
  • full-body photos used for AI Try-On,
  • AI-generated Try-On images,
  • outfit-related images.

Your wardrobe and other locally saved content are primarily stored on your device.

When you use the AI Try-On feature, the selected person image and clothing image are securely transmitted to our cloud processing infrastructure so that the AI-generated image can be created.

AI input images and generated output images are processed transiently for the purpose of completing the requested Try-On. FitCheck does not intentionally write these images to persistent cloud storage as part of the AI generation process.

Generated images may be saved to your device or Photos library only when you choose to do so.

Anonymous Account Information

FitCheck automatically creates an anonymous account when the app is first used.

We may store:

  • an anonymous user identifier,
  • AI credit balance,
  • credit transaction history,
  • subscription status,
  • timestamps associated with credit or account activity.

FitCheck currently does not require users to provide their name, email address, or password in order to use the app.

Purchases and Subscriptions

FitCheck offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple's StoreKit system.

We may receive and store information necessary to validate and manage purchases, including:

  • product identifier,
  • transaction identifier,
  • purchase environment,
  • credit grants associated with a transaction.

Payment information such as your credit card number is processed by Apple and is not provided to FitCheck.

Advertising Information

FitCheck may offer optional rewarded advertisements that users can voluntarily watch in exchange for AI credits.

Advertising is provided through Google AdMob.

Depending on your privacy choices, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • device information,
  • IP address,
  • advertising identifiers where permitted,
  • ad impressions and interactions,
  • diagnostic and performance information.

For users in regions where consent is required, FitCheck uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent before eligible advertisements are requested.

Users may decline consent where applicable. Advertising behavior may differ depending on the user's privacy choices.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through FitCheck is used to:

  • provide wardrobe and outfit functionality,
  • generate AI Try-On images,
  • maintain AI credit balances,
  • validate purchases and subscriptions,
  • prevent duplicate or fraudulent credit transactions,
  • provide optional rewarded advertisements,
  • improve application reliability and security,
  • comply with legal and platform requirements.

We do not sell users' personal photographs.

We do not use uploaded person or clothing photos for advertising.

3. AI Processing

AI Try-On requests are processed on cloud computing infrastructure hosted by Modal using the FASHN VTON model.

Images supplied for AI Try-On are sent to the processing service only when you actively request an AI Try-On.

The images are processed to generate the requested result and are not intentionally stored as permanent image files by FitCheck's AI backend.

Technical infrastructure providers may temporarily process data as necessary to transmit and perform the requested service.

4. Third-Party Services

Supabase

Supabase provides authentication and database infrastructure used for anonymous user accounts, AI credit balances, credit history, and related account information.

Modal

Modal provides cloud computing infrastructure used to perform AI Try-On processing.

Google AdMob

Google AdMob provides optional rewarded advertisements.

Google's processing of advertising data is subject to Google's own privacy policies and the privacy choices presented to the user.

Apple

Apple provides App Store distribution, StoreKit in-app purchases and subscriptions, TestFlight beta distribution, and system services such as Photos access where permitted.

Apple processes payment and App Store account information independently under Apple's privacy policies.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

Some FitCheck information is stored locally on your device, including wardrobe information, outfits, application preferences, and locally retained images.

Deleting this content within the app removes the corresponding locally stored information where that functionality is available.

Deleting FitCheck from your device may remove locally stored application data, subject to Apple's operating system behavior and any device backups you maintain.

6. Data Retention

We retain server-side account, wallet, purchase, and credit transaction information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide FitCheck services,
  • maintain accurate credit balances,
  • prevent fraud or duplicate transactions,
  • comply with legal, accounting, or platform obligations.

AI input and generated images are not intentionally retained as persistent files by FitCheck's AI processing backend after the requested processing is completed.

Some technical logs may be temporarily retained by infrastructure providers for security, reliability, or diagnostic purposes.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • request access to information associated with you,
  • request correction or deletion,
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent,
  • object to or restrict certain processing,
  • manage advertising privacy choices.

Advertising consent can be managed through the privacy options made available by Google UMP where applicable.

To request deletion of server-side information associated with your FitCheck account, contact us using the contact information below.

Certain transaction records may need to be retained where required for fraud prevention, accounting, legal, or platform compliance.

8. Children's Privacy

FitCheck Wardrobe is not specifically designed for children and is not marketed as a children's application.

We do not knowingly request children to provide identifying personal information through FitCheck.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through FitCheck.

Communication with FitCheck's cloud services uses encrypted network connections.

No online service can guarantee absolute security, and users should avoid uploading images or information they do not wish to process through the service.

10. International Processing

FitCheck uses cloud service providers that may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where required, data processing is subject to applicable contractual, legal, and platform safeguards.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when FitCheck's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

When material changes are made, the effective date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.
